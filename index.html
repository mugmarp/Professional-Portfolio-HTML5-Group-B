Who We Are

HAMP DEVELOPERS

Hamp Developers is a team of Software Engineering students at Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST). We build things for Android and for Linux on Android, mostly out of our own frustration with how awkward those two worlds are to combine.

Our current focus is the Lectures Android app, a timetable companion with a write-through offline cache and queued sync so it stays useful with no signal. On the side we work on Termux X11, where we added a floating button that surfaces the soft keyboard on demand while running Linux GUI apps on Android, and on Hamp Keyboard, our fork of the FUTO keyboard with extra spacebar gestures.

Most of us are active in the Mbarara developer community through GDG Mbarara, MUCOSA, AWS Mbarara, BWAT workshops, and Canva meetups. The full roster is on the About page.

Quick Facts

  • Eleven members, all Software Engineering students at MUST
  • Based in Mbarara and Kampala, Uganda
  • Interests: mobile architecture, offline-first systems, Linux/Android internals
  • We work in the open on GitHub
