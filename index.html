I am also exploring customizing or adding features to FOSS apps, like adding a floating keyboard toggle button feature to Termux X11 which surfaces the soft keyboard on demand by just tapping the floating button when running Linux GUI apps on Android.

I am active in the Mbarara developer community through GDG Mbarara, MUCOSA, AWS Mbarara, BWAT workshops, and Canva meetups.
